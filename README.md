## 📱 Smartphone & Mobile Optimized Experience

Since most guests and relatives open wedding links on **Android smartphones and iPhones via WhatsApp**, this website has been built with a **mobile-first app-like design**:

1. **Sticky Bottom Action Dock**:
   - 📍 **Venues & Maps**: Direct 1-tap thumb navigation to wedding destination cards.
   - 📅 **Schedule**: Direct 1-tap jump to auspicious ceremonies & timings.
   - 💬 **Share WhatsApp**: 1-tap to forward formatted wedding details with maps to family WhatsApp groups.
   - 🎵 **Music Toggle**: Easy play/pause control for the auspicious Indian classical Shehnai/Tanpura ambience.
2. **Fluid Typography (`clamp`)**:
   - Names ("Preeti" & "Suryansh") and dates dynamically scale to fit all screen sizes (from iPhone SE 375px to larger Galaxy / Pro Max phones) without breaking words or causing horizontal overflow.
3. **Single-Row Mobile Countdown Timer**:
   - Compact, perfectly aligned countdown boxes that never wrap or break onto multiple lines on phones.
4. **Mobile Navigation Overlay**:
   - Frosted glass backdrop with comfortable touch targets (48px+) and animated hamburger toggle.
5. **Mobile Battery & CPU Optimization**:
   - Petals animation automatically scales down on smartphones and pauses when the user switches apps or minimizes the browser.
